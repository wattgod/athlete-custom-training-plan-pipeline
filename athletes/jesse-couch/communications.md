> Jess,
>
> See attached training pdf.
> As for strength, tell me a bit more about your history/what you've been doing chronically up to this point. Generally strength is like cycling in that you ideally pair your strength phases strategically throughout the year. Where strength needs to be emphasized more is if you have immobilities or strength deficits that prevent you from cycling correctly without pain.
>
> In general, 20 weeks out from your event, if you don't have big strength deficits, you can emphasize strength work more and lift heavier weights for shorter reps if you're adapted as long as its done with great form, generally as safe as possible. So, right now, it's not a big deal if the muscular fatigue of strength training is something you feel on the bike.
>
> As we get closer to race day, say 6 weeks out, you'll want to modify your strength training to a more moderate workout for maintenance and not allow the muscular fatigue to affect you too much on the bike.
>
> Since you're 60, it's more important than ever to be consistent in the gym to keep your strength and mobility sharp as well as giving your body a clear signal that type 2 muscle fibers are something you value.
>
> I hope that helps! Let me know if you have any questions along the way I'm happy to help.
